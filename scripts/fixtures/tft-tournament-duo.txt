==About==
Set 17 is the first year of EMEA's alternative competitive ecosystem structured around Minors and Majors<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://teamfighttactics.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/community/the-future-of-teamfight-tactics-events-in-emea/|title=The Future of Teamfight Tactics Events in EMEA|author=Teamfight Tactics|date=2026-02-20}}</ref>, and the first '''TFT Major EMEA''' is in {{flag|fr}}Paris. The competition will feature four duos representing their respective countries in a unique 2v2v2v2 format.

In addition to the Major, the event will also host fan activities, casual tournaments, and the finals of the {{LeagueIconSmall/spatula tour|link=Space Gods/EMEA/Spatula Tour/4v4 Challenge|name=Spatula Tour French Cup}} '''[[Space Gods/EMEA/Spatula Tour/4v4 Challenge|Spatula Tour 4v4 Challenge]]'''.

===Format===
* 4 teams of 2 compete in a Checkmate 40 Format
** Games are played in Solos game mode with standard scoring.
*** Players score based on their individual placement in each game.
** Once a team scores 40 cummulative points, they are in check.
** When a team has a player score game win after being in check, they win the match.
*** Maximum 6 Games: If no player from a team in check wins Game 6, the champion will be decided by score.
** The {{Bgcolortext|first|winning team}} is awarded and all-expenses-paid qualification to the {{LeagueIconSmall/tft open series}} '''[[TFT Open Series/TFT Vegas Open 2026|TFT Vegas Open 2026]]'''.
