Silent telemetry for better TFT statistics

Waiting for a TFT match

Start a TFT match — the app collects anonymized board-position and composition data in the background.

What we collect

  • Position of your units on the hex board (cell_N)
  • Position of opponent units in lobby fights (cell_N)
  • Match-ID, region, your final placement at match-end
  • Augments you picked — used for statistics only, never displayed

What we do NOT do

  • No in-game advice or coaching
  • No augment recommendations (Riot ToS)
  • No memory reading, no Vanguard interference
  • No personal data shared with third parties
