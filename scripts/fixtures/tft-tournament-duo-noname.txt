==About==
===Format===
* '''Double Up:''' ''May 9<sup>th</sup> - 10<sup>th</sup>''
** 4 squads are split into ''4 Upper Bracket Duos'' and ''4 Lower Bracket Duos''.
** Each breaket plays 8 games across ''Day 1 & Day 2''.
** Games 13-16 have doubled point values.
** {{Bgcolortext|up|Top 1<sup>st</sup> team}} advance to the '''4v4 Bracket - Grand Finals'''.
** {{Bgcolortext|stayup|Top 2<sup>nd</sup> team}} advance to the '''4v4 Bracket - Semifinals'''.
** {{Bgcolortext|staydown|Top 3<sup>rd</sup> & Top 4<sup>th</sup> teams}} advance to the '''4v4 Bracket - Quarterfinals'''.

* '''4v4 Bracket:''' ''May 11<sup>th</sup>''
** '''Quarterfinals:'''
*** 2 teams play {{Abbr/Bo3}}.
*** Winning team advannces to '''Semifinals.'''
** '''Semifinals:'''
*** 2 teams play {{Abbr/Bo3}}.
*** Winning team advannces to '''Grand Finals.'''
** '''Grand Finals:'''
*** 2 teams play {{Abbr/Bo5}}.
*** Winning team will be the champions of '''TFT Digi Duos Invitational.'''
