==About==
The '''Esports World Cup''' is a tournament series that was announced in late 2023, building off the previously established '''Gamers8''' series. '''Teamfight Tactics''' was announced as part of the line-up on February 2, 2025.<ref name="EWC TFT">{{cite web|url=https://x.com/EWC_EN/status/1889034098909561267|title=Time to start a Riot|date=2025-02-10|author=Esports World Cup|language={{FlagNoLink|usuk}} English}}</ref>
:''See [https://liquipedia.net/esports/Esports_World_Cup/2025 here] to read about the Esports World Cup 2025, the Calendar, the Club Standings and more.''

===Format===
* '''Group Stage:''' ''August 11 - 13''
** 16 teams are split into 4 even groups of 4 teams.
** For each group, 4 teams compete in the Double-Elimination (GSL) format.
*** All matches are {{Abbr/Bo3}}.
*** {{Abbr/Top2}} teams from each group advance to the ''Playoffs''.

*'''Playoffs:''' ''August 14 - 15''
** 8 teams qualified from ''Group Stage'' compete in the Single-Elimination format.
*** All matches ''(excl. Grand Final)'' are {{Abbr/Bo3}}.
*** Grand Final is {{Abbr/Bo5}}.
